# EU / Netherlands launch checklist

Status: **engineering draft — no public token offer is authorized by this document.**

AIHF is designed toward utility-token characteristics: access to issuer-provided software/data services, with no stable-value claim and no profit/equity right. Final legal classification must be made from the complete facts at launch.

## Before any public EU offer or admission to trading

1. Establish the responsible legal person and issuer/offeror roles.
2. Obtain counsel review of MiCA classification and check whether another EU financial-services regime applies.
3. Confirm the product services attached to the token exist or define the launch timeline carefully.
4. Prepare the MiCA crypto-asset whitepaper when required, including project, rights/obligations, technology, risks and environmental disclosures.
5. Include all mandatory Article 6 statements and warnings in their legally required wording.
6. Prepare the explanation why the asset is neither an ART nor EMT and why it is not otherwise excluded/reclassified.
7. Notify the competent home-state authority within the required period when notification applies.
8. Publish the final notified version and aligned marketing materials before the offer/admission as required.
9. Maintain versioned archives and change-control for whitepaper and marketing.
10. Keep all marketing fair, clear and non-misleading and make no unsupported future-value claim.
11. Complete smart-contract audit, source verification, treasury multisig and vesting.
12. Document complaints, incidents, conflicts, treasury transactions, liquidity policy and service continuity.
13. Do not advertise a future CEX/DEX listing as guaranteed.
14. Reassess CASP obligations before providing custody, exchange, execution, placing, portfolio management or similar services for others.

## Current engineering assumptions

- not a euro/USD/stable-value token;
- not asset-referenced;
- not a share, debt instrument, fund unit or right to AutoTrader PnL;
- no interest or staking yield;
- no public offer during testnet;
- no mainnet liquidity during testnet.

## Netherlands

AFM is the relevant Dutch authority for MiCA Title II whitepapers for crypto-assets other than ARTs and EMTs. AFM currently states that whitepaper submissions themselves have no filing fee. This does not remove legal, drafting, audit or operational costs.

## Exemption warning

MiCA contains exemptions for some offers, including certain small/private offers and some utility tokens for existing services, but exemptions can cease to apply when an intention to seek admission to trading is communicated. Do not build the launch around an exemption without legal review.

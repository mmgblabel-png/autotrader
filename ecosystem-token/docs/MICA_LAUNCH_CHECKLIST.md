# EU / Netherlands launch checklist

Status: **engineering/compliance gate — no public token offer, admission to trading, DEX liquidity or marketing campaign is authorised by this document.**

AIHF is engineered toward utility-token functionality: access to issuer-provided software/data services, without a stable-value claim, dividend, profit share, equity right or claim on AutoTrader NAV. This is a design objective, **not a legal classification**. Final classification must be performed from the complete product, distribution and marketing facts immediately before launch.

## Hard gate before any public EU offer or admission to trading

The following items are release blockers, not suggestions:

1. Establish the responsible legal person and identify issuer, offeror and any person seeking admission to trading.
2. Obtain specialist legal review of MiCA classification and check whether AIHF or any surrounding service falls under another EU financial-services regime.
3. Decide whether there will actually be an offer to the public, admission to trading, or both. Do not use vague future-listing language as a substitute for this decision.
4. Confirm that the product services attached to AIHF exist and operate. If an offer concerns a utility token giving access to goods/services that do not yet exist or are not yet operational, legal counsel must specifically review MiCA Article 4(6), including its 12-month limit for such an offer.
5. Prepare the Title II crypto-asset white paper when required, including project, offer/admission, token functionality, rights/obligations, technology, risks and sustainability disclosures.
6. Include the mandatory Article 6 statements and warnings in the legally required form.
7. **Do not make assertions about AIHF's future value.** MiCA Article 6 restricts future-value assertions in the white paper.
8. Document that AIHF may lose value, may not always be transferable/liquid, and—if classified as a utility token—that access to the promised service can fail if the project fails/discontinues, as required by Article 6 where applicable.
9. Notify the competent home-state authority within the applicable Title II notification period and format when notification is required.
10. Publish the notified white paper and aligned marketing communications before the offer/admission as required, and keep the current version publicly accessible for the required period.
11. Keep versioned archives of superseded white papers and marketing material.
12. Keep all marketing fair, clear and non-misleading and consistent with the white paper.
13. Complete independent smart-contract audit/review, verified source builds, treasury multisig, key-separation review, vesting funding verification and incident-response rehearsal.
14. Document complaints, conflicts, treasury transactions, liquidity policy, service continuity and cyber/security ownership.
15. Do not advertise a CEX/DEX listing, liquidity level, resale value, token price or return as guaranteed.
16. Reassess CASP obligations before providing custody, exchange, execution, placing, reception/transmission of orders, advice, portfolio management, transfer services or operating a trading platform for others.
17. Obtain a written release decision from legal/compliance and security owners before enabling mainnet distribution or liquidity.

## Current engineering assumptions

These assumptions are intentionally conservative and must be re-tested before launch:

- AIHF is not designed to reference EUR, USD, another official currency or a basket of assets;
- it is not designed as an ART or EMT;
- it does not create a share, debt instrument, fund unit or right to AutoTrader PnL/NAV;
- there is no interest, staking yield, dividend or protocol revenue share;
- AIHF access is denominated in fixed token units, not token market value;
- no public offer occurs during Polygon Amoy testing;
- no mainnet liquidity occurs during testnet;
- AutoTrader live trading capital is never used for token launch, LAND, Catalysts, Founder Key minting, marketing or liquidity;
- Sandbox passes and Founder Keys are separate product/access credentials and do not create financial rights in AutoTrader.

## Netherlands / AFM gate

For a Netherlands-led launch, confirm home Member State with counsel.

Current AFM guidance states that:
- AFM handles submissions of Title II white papers for crypto-assets other than ARTs and EMTs in the Netherlands;
- Title II white papers are **not approved** by the authority; they are notified, so marketing must not call a notified white paper “MiCAR-compliant” merely because it was submitted;
- AFM currently charges no fee for white-paper notification/approval itself;
- “seeking admission to trading” means a concrete intention involving an identified trading platform, not a merely hypothetical future possibility;
- marketing communications must remain consistent with the relevant white paper;
- provision of crypto-asset services may create separate CASP licensing/notification obligations.

## Sandbox / metaverse marketing gate

The HQ and Founder Key marketing must follow the same truthfulness boundary:
- describe AIHF as access/utility, not an investment;
- show Reader / Pro / Quant features rather than speculative token value;
- no “earn”, “yield”, “profit share”, “guaranteed floor”, “guaranteed resale” or similar claims;
- no gamified quest may require a user to buy AIHF merely to complete the public educational path;
- premium rooms may advertise actual services/perks, but they must not imply stronger trading returns;
- Founder Key scarcity must be stated only after its final production supply is frozen.

## Exemption warning

MiCA contains exemptions for certain offers. Those exemptions are fact-specific and can be lost—for example, Article 4 states that certain offer exemptions do not apply where an intention to seek admission to trading is communicated. Do not design the commercial launch around an exemption without written legal review.

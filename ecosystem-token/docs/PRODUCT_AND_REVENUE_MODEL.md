# Product and revenue model

## Product thesis

AIHF exists to unlock **software, data and virtual-experience utility**, not to represent an investment in AutoTrader.

The token is an access credential that can be locked to unlock increasingly capable products around market research, strategy analytics, developer tooling and the AI HedgeFund HQ in The Sandbox. The core product must be useful even if AIHF has no secondary-market price.

## Product surfaces

### Public
- methodology and risk-policy documentation;
- product status and transparency pages;
- delayed educational material;
- Sandbox Public Lobby;
- treasury/ecosystem visualisation;
- gamified onboarding quests.

### Reader
- research feed;
- historical research archive;
- market watchlists;
- portfolio/risk education;
- Sandbox Research Floor;
- Sandbox Shadow Agent / Performance Room.

### Pro
- premium dashboard;
- lower-delay research;
- strategy performance analytics;
- alerts;
- downloadable datasets/reports;
- Sandbox Pro Strategy Lab.

### Quant
- real-time research entitlement;
- high-rate data/API access;
- advanced strategy lab;
- marketplace creator tooling;
- non-binding research-signal voting;
- Sandbox Quant Vault.

The canonical machine-readable entitlement set is in `config/access-catalog.json`. The Sandbox-specific mirror is in `config/sandbox-hq-access.json`.

## Sandbox Monetized Empire

The owned LAND is developed as **MMinc AI HedgeFund HQ**.

The HQ combines:
- public educational discovery;
- wallet-authenticated AIHF entitlement;
- native Sandbox NFT Sensor gating where appropriate;
- tier presentation passes;
- a separate scarce MMinc Founder Key;
- quests and non-pay-to-win perks;
- portals to live AIHF research/dashboard services;
- a later strategy marketplace.

Sensitive premium access is always enforced by the live AIHF vault entitlement outside The Sandbox. A Sandbox pass is a presentation/gating credential, not an investment claim and not a substitute for live authorization.

## Revenue model

The business should monetize **services and experiences**, not promises of token appreciation.

Permitted product models for legal review:
1. fiat/stable-value subscription for premium software;
2. AIHF lock as an alternative entitlement path;
3. enterprise/API contracts priced independently of token market value;
4. Founder Key and non-pay-to-win Sandbox NFT/asset sales;
5. clearly labeled sponsored educational exhibits or events;
6. marketplace service fees on actual software/data transactions, subject to legal/CASP review.

Explicitly excluded:
- promised investment return;
- percentage of AutoTrader profit;
- dividend;
- redemption against fund NAV;
- guaranteed buybacks;
- guaranteed secondary-market liquidity;
- token-price targets;
- pay-to-win combat/stat advantages;
- using AutoTrader live bankroll for LAND, token launch, liquidity or marketing.

## Founder Key

The MMinc Founder Key is a separate scarce NFT product. It can provide:
- access to the Founder Room;
- founder lore/quests;
- cosmetics and badges;
- recognition;
- priority beta invitations.

It cannot bypass Reader/Pro/Quant requirements for sensitive research, data or APIs.

## Strategy marketplace

The marketplace distributes software licences, research packs, indicators, templates and strategy configurations. It must not automatically grant third parties the ability to place trades from the user's exchange account.

A listing record should contain:
- creator identifier;
- immutable strategy/version hash;
- supported venue/markets;
- methodology summary;
- backtest window and data provenance;
- fee/slippage assumptions;
- live/shadow evidence where available;
- risk characteristics;
- licence terms;
- price and payment method;
- security review state.

Execution-capable code requires a separate sandbox/security approval before it can interact with AutoTrader.

## Research budget signaling

QUANT users may sign non-binding votes on research priorities, for example:
- which markets receive simulation budget;
- which execution topics receive engineering attention;
- which datasets/integrations should be evaluated.

V1 votes do **not** move treasury funds and do not control the company, AutoTrader bankroll or risk mandate. Any future binding financial governance requires a new legal and security review.

## Separation from AutoTrader

AIHF, Sandbox HQ and their treasury/operating budgets are outside the live fund accounting boundary:
- AIHF treasury balance is not NAV;
- AIHF is not collateral;
- AutoTrader never auto-buys AIHF;
- token price never changes strategy sizing;
- token holders have no claim on trading profits;
- launch/liquidity spending cannot come from the live trading bankroll;
- LAND, Catalysts, Founder Key minting and marketing cannot use AutoTrader live trading capital.

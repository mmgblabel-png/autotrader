# AI HedgeFund Access Token — engineering whitepaper draft

**Document status:** pre-legal, pre-notification engineering draft. It is not an approved, notified or public-offer whitepaper.

## 1. Project

AI HedgeFund is a software and research ecosystem around algorithmic market analysis, risk analytics, strategy research and portfolio tooling. AIHF is intended to provide access to services supplied by the ecosystem operator.

## 2. Crypto-asset

- Working name: AI HedgeFund Access Token
- Working ticker: AIHF
- Standard: ERC-20
- Intended network: Polygon PoS
- Fixed supply: 100,000,000 AIHF
- Decimals: 18
- Minting after deployment: impossible in the contract
- Stable-value mechanism: none
- Yield mechanism: none
- Rights to company/fund profits or assets: none

## 3. Utility

Intended utilities:
- Reader research access;
- Pro premium dashboard/research/alerts;
- Quant advanced strategy-lab/API/marketplace tooling when operational.

Locking tokens in the access vault provides a machine-verifiable entitlement. The vault pays no reward or interest.

## 4. Technology

The token and vault use Solidity and OpenZeppelin primitives on an EVM-compatible Polygon network. The token is non-upgradeable. The access vault is non-yielding and has no administrator.

Mainnet treasury operations are intended to use a multisig Safe. Insider vesting must be enforced before public distribution.

## 5. Supply and distribution

See TOKENOMICS.md. The fixed supply is initially delivered to the launch treasury and is then distributed under a public allocation and vesting policy.

## 6. Holder rights and limitations

A token holder may transfer AIHF subject to network availability and may lock it in the access vault. Product access depends on the locked amount and continued operation of the relevant service.

Holding AIHF does not create:
- equity;
- debt;
- a claim against AutoTrader NAV;
- a dividend or revenue share;
- guaranteed redemption;
- guaranteed liquidity;
- a guaranteed listing;
- a guaranteed future market value.

## 7. Principal risks

Risks include complete or partial loss of market value, illiquidity, wallet compromise, smart-contract defects, blockchain disruption, service changes/discontinuation, regulatory reclassification, cyber incidents and third-party infrastructure failures.

## 8. Environmental information

The final compliance whitepaper must include the required environmental/adverse-impact disclosure for the consensus mechanism and network using the applicable MiCA technical format.

## 9. Issuer/offeror information

No public offer is enabled at this engineering stage. The final document must identify the responsible legal person, registered address, management body, contact channels and all other legally required fields before a public offer or admission to trading.

## 10. Regulatory completion gate

Before this becomes a public-offer whitepaper, counsel must map every required MiCA Annex I field, insert the exact statutory first-page and risk statements, complete the notification rationale and establish version-controlled publication procedures.

No statement in this engineering draft predicts or promises AIHF's future value.

## Selection rule

The eventual shortlist should favor clear topical relevance, conservative claim language, transparent affiliate terms, owner-approved active eligibility, product-page quality, and measurable first-party tracking. It must not select campaigns solely on headline commission, PayPro score, or promised outcomes.

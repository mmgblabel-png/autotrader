## Product-page verification

### Roku Streaming Stick HD — leading test candidate

Amazon’s product page identifies the item as **Roku Streaming Stick HD with Voice Remote**, ASIN `B0DXXYS4BJ`, in `Electronics › Television & Video › Streaming Media Players`. The Associates SiteStripe displayed **Home Entertainment** as the commission category and **4.00%** as the commission rate at review time. The live page presented it as a 1080p TV-stick form factor with HDMI, Bluetooth/Wi-Fi connectivity, voice remote control, and current compatible-service / smart-home statements.

Only the following stable facts are appropriate for a first draft, subject to a final listing check: the HD form factor; 1080p resolution; HDMI connection; remote/voice-control interface; compact device that plugs into the back of a TV; and that it uses wireless connectivity. Drafts must not repeat the current price, offers, stock, rating, review count, Amazon’s Choice label, comparative platform-ranking claim, content availability, service catalog, setup speed, smart-home compatibility, or delivery claim. The product was **not shippable to the current Netherlands delivery location**; this reinforces a US audience restriction but does not itself prove US delivery eligibility for every user.

### Sony MDR-ZX110 wired on-ear headphones — secondary fallback

The Sony MDR-ZX110 search listing was visible as an Electronics best seller with a low offer price. Search results indicated no featured offer was available for the viewed black variant. Because the search page is not a single current seller offer and the item has many variants/offer conditions, it is not selected for immediate promotion. It can be researched later as an inexpensive, simple-use-case fallback, but it should not be promoted without a stable exact listing, current delivery verification, and a valid Special Link.

### Candidate decision

**Select Roku Streaming Stick HD (ASIN B0DXXYS4BJ) as the electronics research candidate, not as a proven conversion winner.** Its inclusion in the Electronics top ten, accessible offer level, concrete form factor, and familiar category support a controlled US-only test. The category’s 24-hour momentum list had no usable items at review time, the account has no historical conversions, and the product page includes compatibility-sensitive statements; all three facts require conservative copy and post-test measurement.

## Official source corroboration and promotion restrictions

Roku’s official product page identifies the HD Streaming Stick as model `3840R`. It describes up-to-1080p HD output, dual-band Wi-Fi, built-in HDMI and USB-C for power, a voice remote with television power/volume/mute controls, and an HDMI-equipped television requirement. The configured campaign will use a conservative subset of those facts and will require viewers to verify their own TV, network, content-service, and regional requirements before purchasing. Roku’s page also shows temporary price, offer, review, and platform-leadership content; none of that material should be copied into the campaign.

Amazon’s current standard commission table lists **All Other Categories** at 4.00%; the current SiteStripe panel on the Amazon Roku listing also displayed Home Entertainment at 4.00%. This supports the observed rate only as an internal planning input—never as a public earnings promise or a permanent category assertion.

Amazon’s Participation Requirements require Associate content to link to the relevant Amazon detail page, prohibit customer review/star-rating reuse without permitted API use, prohibit paid-search placements that take users directly or indirectly through a redirect to Amazon, prohibit incentives and artificial click generation, prohibit customer-account data collection, prohibit orders on behalf of others, prohibit pop-up/pop-under or transitional-page promotion, and prohibit cloaking or obscuring the URL of a site containing Special Links. Critically, Section 6 also prohibits offline promotion and use of Special Links in email, SMS/MMS, or email attachments. Therefore this Amazon campaign must **exclude email and SMS from the direct-link promotion workflow**. This requirement supersedes the generic email-draft capability inherited from the prior automation.
